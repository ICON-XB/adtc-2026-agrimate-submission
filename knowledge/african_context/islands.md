# African Islands Agricultural Context

## Madagascar
- **Key Crops**: Vanilla, cloves, rice, cassava, and coffee.
- **Challenges**: Cyclones frequently devastate crops; severe deforestation and soil erosion; locust swarms.
- **Practices**: System of Rice Intensification (SRI) was developed here to increase yields with less water and fewer seeds.

## Mauritius & Reunion (Indian Ocean)
- **Key Crops**: Sugarcane, tea, and tropical fruits (lychee, pineapple).
- **Challenges**: Limited arable land, vulnerability to powerful tropical cyclones, and shifting global sugar prices.
- **Practices**: Highly mechanized sugarcane farming; increasing diversification into smart agriculture and hydroponics.

## Comoros & Seychelles
- **Key Crops**: Ylang-ylang (essential oils), vanilla, cloves, and coconuts.
- **Challenges**: Volcanic soils can be steep and hard to farm (Comoros); extremely limited land mass and heavy reliance on food imports (Seychelles).
- **Practices**: Agroforestry and spice gardens; strict biosecurity to protect island ecosystems.

## Cape Verde & São Tomé and Príncipe (Atlantic Ocean)
- **Key Crops**: Cocoa and coffee (São Tomé), maize, beans, and bananas (Cape Verde).
- **Challenges**: Extreme drought and volcanic rocky terrain in Cape Verde; aging cocoa trees and fungal diseases in São Tomé.
- **Practices**: Drip irrigation and desalinization for water (Cape Verde); shade-grown organic cocoa production (São Tomé).
